---
name: History tab visibility
description: Keep the two filters on “Mon relevé” visibly distinct and accessible.
---

Both “Tout” and “Revenus” filters must remain visible on the history page. The selected filter uses the gold treatment; the unselected filter keeps a dark, bordered background with readable gold text.

**Why:** the user reported that both buttons blended into the page and were difficult to see, even after a prior CSS adjustment.

**How to apply:** when changing history-page styles, verify the final cascade for both active and inactive states, and keep the tab controls above decorative page backgrounds.
