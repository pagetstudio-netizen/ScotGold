---
name: Daily check-in bonus
description: Eligibility and accounting rules for the daily presence reward.
---

The homepage presence button awards a 50 FCFA login bonus once per calendar day. A repeated claim on the same day must not credit the account again and should tell the user to return tomorrow. Keep this reward separate from fortune-wheel turns and product earnings.

**Why:** the user explicitly requested a 50 FCFA check-in bonus with a once-daily claim limit.

**How to apply:** enforce claims on the server, record the reward as its own transaction, and credit the unified spendable balance without changing product earnings or wheel eligibility.
