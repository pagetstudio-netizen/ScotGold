name: Product payout policy
description: The agreed earnings policy for purchased stable and activity products.
---

Stable products credit their configured daily earnings to the withdrawal balance after each elapsed 24-hour cycle; do this for existing stable positions as well as new purchases. Never exceed the position’s saved total return, and credit only once per elapsed cycle. Activity products keep their maturity payout. The homepage lists stable offers only; preserve existing activity positions and their records. Free daily check-in bonuses are separate from product earnings.

**Why:** the user explicitly changed the policy on October 27, 2026: existing stable-product gains must be credited every 24 hours, and the homepage must no longer show stable/activity category buttons.

**How to apply:** use the saved purchase type and daily return, not later catalog edits; keep each payout and its position update atomic so missed cycles cannot be double-credited.
